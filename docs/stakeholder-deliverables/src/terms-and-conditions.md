## 1. Acceptance of terms

By creating an account, signing in, or using the Bainum platform ("Platform"),
you agree to these Terms and Conditions ("Terms"). If you do not agree, do not
use the Platform.

## 2. Eligibility and accounts

2.1. Accounts are issued to program administrators, teachers, and parents
participating in authorized early childhood programs.

2.2. You must provide accurate registration information and keep credentials
confidential. You are responsible for activity under your account.

2.3. Parents may register only through valid invitation links issued by the
program.

## 3. Permitted use

3.1. Use the Platform only for legitimate educational assessment, recording, and
collaboration related to enrolled children.

3.2. Administrators and teachers may manage schools, classrooms, and recordings
according to their assigned roles.

3.3. Parents may view and contribute data only for their own linked children.

## 4. Prohibited conduct

You must not:

- Access or attempt to access another user's data without authorization.
- Upload unlawful, abusive, or unrelated content.
- Interfere with Platform security or availability.
- Reverse engineer or scrape the service except as permitted by law.
- Share login credentials or invitation links publicly.

## 5. Child data privacy and confidentiality

5.1. The Platform processes sensitive information about children, including
audio recordings, transcripts, and developmental analytics.

5.2. Users must treat all child data as confidential and use it only for
educational purposes within the program.

5.3. Parents may access full assessment data only for their own children.
Teachers may access child data only for children they supervise or for whom an
active access grant exists. Administrators may access data as required for program
operation.

5.4. Classroom rosters may display children's names to enrolled parents and
staff; full records remain role-scoped.

## 6. Data collection and use

6.1. The Platform collects account information, child profiles, audio
recordings, transcripts, location and activity labels, and usage metadata
necessary to operate the service.

6.2. Audio is processed to produce transcripts and keyword or semantic analyses
for developmental tracking.

6.3. Aggregated or de-identified statistics may be used for program evaluation
and research only as permitted by program policy and applicable law.

## 7. Third-party services

7.1. The Platform relies on external providers for speech transcription,
optional AI-based analysis, email delivery, and cloud hosting.

7.2. Data sent to those providers is limited to what is required for the
requested feature. Provider terms and security practices apply to their processing.

7.3. The program does not sell child personal information to third parties for
marketing.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

8.1. Transcripts are associated with an expiry date (currently **365 days** after
the recording date) after which transcript text may be purged from active storage
while other assessment metadata may remain.

8.2. In-app notifications expire automatically after a short retention window
(approximately ten days).

8.3. Account deletion and broader data deletion requests are handled according
to program policy and applicable law; contact the program administrator.

## 9. Usability testing and research participation

Participation in moderated usability sessions is voluntary and separate from
routine Platform use. See the **Bainum Usability Testing Protocol** for consent
procedures, recording policies, and participant rights before conducting or
joining a session.
