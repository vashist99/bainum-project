# Bainum Platform — Usability Testing Protocol

**Version:** 1.1 | **Date:** June 2026  
**Facilitator materials for moderated sessions with teachers and parents**

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This protocol guides **moderated usability sessions** for the Bainum early
childhood platform. Sessions evaluate whether non-technical teachers and parents
can complete core tasks without assistance, and capture qualitative feedback for
navigation and labeling improvements (including future mobile navigation work).

## 2. Session logistics

| Item | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| Duration | 45–60 minutes per participant |
| Method | Moderated think-aloud; facilitator reads tasks aloud |
| Environment | Quiet room, stable Wi-Fi, desktop or participant's phone |
| Recording | Optional screen/audio recording only with documented consent |
| Roles | Facilitator (leads), Observer (notes), Participant |
| Materials | Test accounts, this protocol, observation worksheet, consent forms |

## 3. Participant criteria

### Teachers

- Active teacher account.
- At least one classroom as lead or assistant, OR willingness to create a test
  classroom in a sandbox environment.

### Parents

- Accepted parent invitation and at least one linked child.
- Optional: enrolled in at least one classroom for read-only classroom tasks.

## 4. Consent checklist (before session)

- [ ] Participant read or received summary of **Terms and Conditions (Draft)**.
- [ ] Verbal and/or written consent obtained for session participation.
- [ ] If recording: separate recording consent obtained (scope and retention explained).
- [ ] Participant knows they may stop at any time without penalty.

## 5. Teacher task scenarios

Read the **Task prompt** aloud. Do not help unless the participant is stuck for
**3 minutes**; then offer the **Allowed hint**. Score using the observation
worksheet.

### Task T1 — Open classroom homepage

- **Prompt:** "Show me how you would open one of your classrooms."
- **Start:** Dashboard after sign-in.
- **Success:** Classroom homepage loads with name, school, and teacher info visible.
- **Hint:** "Use the classroom card on your dashboard."

### Task T2 — Add parents to classroom

- **Prompt:** "A new parent needs to be added to this classroom. Walk me through how you would do that."
- **Start:** Classroom homepage (lead or assistant).
- **Success:** Add Parents modal opens; parent selected; Add confirmed; success feedback shown.
- **Hint:** "Look for the button that says Add Parents."

### Task T3 — Record activity with location and activity

- **Prompt:** "Record or upload an activity for a child, including where it happened and what type of activity it was."
- **Start:** Dashboard or classroom homepage.
- **Success:** Location and activity selected; audio submitted; processing started.
- **Hint:** "Use Record Activity and pick from the dropdown lists."

### Task T4 — Find a child's transcript

- **Prompt:** "Find the most recent transcript for [named child]."
- **Start:** Children list or classroom homepage.
- **Success:** Participant opens child or classroom transcripts and identifies latest entry (listed at the **top**; lists are sorted newest first).
- **Hint:** "Try the Children page or transcripts section on the classroom page — the newest transcript is first."

### Task T5 — Use notification bell

- **Prompt:** "Check whether you have any notifications and open one if present."
- **Start:** Any authenticated page with navbar visible.
- **Success:** Bell opened; notification list viewed; optional navigation on click
  (classroom for added/notes/recordings, child page for child notes, or home for
  removal events).
- **Hint:** "Look at the top bar for the bell icon."

### Task T6 — Add a classroom note

- **Prompt:** "Add a short note on the classroom page that parents can read."
- **Start:** Classroom homepage (lead or assistant teacher).
- **Success:** Note appears in Notes & Observations; enrolled parent can see it
  read-only.
- **Hint:** "Scroll to Notes & Observations on the classroom page."

### Task T7 — Filter children by school (admin)

- **Prompt:** "Show me only children associated with a specific school."
- **Start:** Children page (admin).
- **Success:** Participant uses **Filter by School** dropdown correctly.
- **Hint:** "Use the Filter by School dropdown near the top."

### Task T8 — Teacher supervised children list

- **Prompt:** "Show me the children you are responsible for as a teacher."
- **Start:** Children page after teacher sign-in.
- **Success:** Participant opens **Children** and sees supervised roster (may note
  school context label but list is not incorrectly empty).
- **Hint:** "Use Children in the sidebar."

## 6. Parent task scenarios

### Task P1 — Open child data page

- **Prompt:** "Open your child's main data page where you see progress and recordings."
- **Start:** Dashboard after parent sign-in.
- **Success:** Child data page loads with charts or recording history visible.
- **Hint:** "Your child's name or card on the home screen."

### Task P2 — View developmental information

- **Prompt:** "Find where you can see how your child is doing in language or development charts."
- **Start:** Child data page.
- **Success:** Participant identifies chart or assessment section.
- **Hint:** "Scroll to the charts or assessment area on your child's page."

### Task P3 — Read-only classroom view

- **Prompt:** "Open a classroom where your child is enrolled and tell me what you can see there."
- **Start:** Dashboard (**My Children's Classrooms** cards).
- **Success:** Read-only classroom opens; participant notes no staff edit buttons;
  classroom notes visible without add/delete controls; child names in the roster
  link to child data pages.
- **Hint:** "Open a classroom card from your dashboard."

### Task P4 — View notes and notifications

- **Prompt:** "Find any notes about your child or classroom, check the bell for
  new note or recording alerts, and open your child's page from the classroom
  roster if shown."
- **Start:** Child data page or classroom homepage.
- **Success:** Notes section located; bell opened; optional navigation from a note
  notification OR participant states none present.
- **Hint:** "Look for Notes & Observations and the bell in the top bar."

### Task P5 — Dismiss a notification

- **Prompt:** "If you have a notification, view it and clear it from your list."
- **Start:** Any page with bell visible.
- **Success:** Notification opened and dismissed OR participant states none present.
- **Hint:** "Use the bell in the top navigation bar."

### Task P6 — Parent sidebar navigation

- **Prompt:** "Use the sidebar to go to your child's main data page and back to the dashboard."
- **Start:** Any authenticated parent page.
- **Success:** Participant uses **My Child's Data** and **Dashboard**; notes there
  is no separate **Classrooms** menu item (classrooms live on the dashboard).
- **Hint:** "Look at the left sidebar for Dashboard and My Child's Data."

## 7. Mobile navigation debrief (optional)

If the participant used a phone or narrow browser window, ask:

1. Was the sidebar easy to find (hamburger menu)?
2. Did you know which section you were in?
3. What would make navigation easier on a small screen?

Record answers in observer notes for product planning.

## 9. Post-session questionnaire

Ask after tasks (Likert: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree):

1. I could find the main features I needed without help.
2. Labels such as Schools, Children, and Add Parents made sense to me.
3. I felt confident viewing my (or my students') data.
4. The notification bell was easy to understand.
5. Overall, I am satisfied with the platform's ease of use.

**Open-ended:**

1. What was the most confusing moment in today's session?
2. What one change would help you most next week?

## 10. Facilitator wrap-up

- Thank the participant.
- Remind them that usability findings improve the product for all users.
- Store observation worksheet and questionnaire per program data policy.
- Do not share other participants' recordings without authorization.
